MINT — with a mint leaf

Uniform-height connected two-tone wordmark, with a leaf worked in a few different ways. Leaf is a simple two-lobe mint sprig with a center vein.

Option A — Sprig on the I

The shared center vertical (the I) grows a small two-leaf sprig out the top — the join literally “sprouts”.

Option B — Leaf on the T

The right end of the T crossbar curls up into a single leaf, like a stem with a fresh tip. Subtle and grown-up.

Option C — Leaf in the M

A leaf tucks into the negative space of the M’s valley — hidden until you look. Keeps the wordmark clean.

Option D — Leaf badge

App-icon style: the connected mark sits inside a rounded tile, with a leaf sprouting off the I above it. Great as a favicon / avatar.
