Gannomat 1080 Drilling MachineClosed and CompletedHannah French Hannah French - 01/20/26 5:22:21 AM: When you encounter this error code it usually comes from the machine not being shut down properly through the shut down process, but that's not always the cause. If you see this error, close the window and choose the app "recover indexLS21" on the right side of the screen. It will give you a prompt box. Hit enter, and hit enter again. It should automatically close this window. You next need to select "IndexLS21" app on the left hand side of the screen. This will bring your programs back up. You will need to do a complete shut down. When it comes back up- More likely than not, your tool will display in red -1 and a stop sign. This prevents the machine from doing any drilling tasks. You have to manually go in to the tooling program (F2) edit, then (F7) for tools. You have to delete the 8 in the first box and change it to a 1 and press enter. Change the next box to a 1 as well and hit enter. (F8) to back all the way out of edit options until you are back on the regular scanning screen. Must do another complete shutdown before running. As a sidenote- I do not think the operators should be messing with programming. 0.000.008.001/20/2026
