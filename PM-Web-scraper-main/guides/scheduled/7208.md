# Scheduled Maintenance Procedure: Robotic Welder (EQ ID 1961)

## Overview
Greasing the mechanical unit of the Lincoln Electric robotic welder keeps the drive gears, linear bearings, and joint bearings lubricated, preventing premature wear, excessive heat buildup, and unexpected motion stoppages. Proper lubrication ensures repeatable welding accuracy, reduces downtime, and extends the service life of the robot’s motion system.

## Tools & Parts Needed
- Safety glasses and cut‑resistant gloves  
- Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) kit (padlock, hasp, tags)  
- Standard hand tools (8 mm and 10 mm socket/wrench set, 5 mm and 6 mm Allen keys)  
- Lithium complex grease (NLGI grade 2) supplied in a grease gun with compatible fitting adapter  
- Lint‑free cleaning rags  
- Small plastic scraper (optional, for old grease removal)  

## Safety
- **Power‑down**: Shut off the main power breaker for the robot and the controller.  
- **LOTO**: Apply lockout devices to both the main breaker and the robot’s emergency‑stop circuit. Verify zero voltage with a multimeter before proceeding.  
- **Interlocks**: Open and secure any safety interlock doors according to the OEM safety diagram; do not operate the robot while the access door is open.  
- **Personal Protection**: Wear safety glasses and cut‑resistant gloves to protect against grease splatter and sharp edges.  

## Step‑By‑Step Procedure
1. Confirm the work order and that the robot is in **Manual/Teach** mode with the arm positioned at its home (park) location.  
2. Turn OFF the main power breaker for the robotic welder and the controller.  
3. Attach LOTO devices to the breaker and the emergency‑stop circuit; verify the absence of voltage.  
4. Open the **Safety Interlock Door** on the robot base by releasing the interlock latch and removing the securing pins.  
5. Remove the **Mechanical Unit Access Plate** (four 8 mm bolts) using the appropriate socket wrench and set the bolts aside in a clean tray.  
6. Locate all grease fittings on the robot’s mechanical unit:  
   - X‑axis drive gear housing  
   - Y‑axis linear bearing housings  
   - Z‑axis ball‑screw bearings  
   - Wrist roll, pitch, and yaw gearboxes  
7. Using a lint‑free rag, **clean each fitting area** to remove old grease, metal shavings, or dust.  
8. Connect the grease gun to the first fitting and **pump grease until it begins to ooze from the bearing lips**, indicating that the internal passage is full.  
9. Manually **rotate or jog the associated joint through its full range of motion** (≈2‑3 cycles) to distribute the fresh grease throughout the bearing surfaces.  
10. Wipe any excess grease from the exterior of the fitting with a clean rag.  
11. Repeat steps 8‑10 for **each remaining grease fitting** on the mechanical unit.  
12. Visually inspect all joints for **smooth movement** and ensure no grease is leaking onto adjacent electrical components.  
13. Re‑install the Mechanical Unit Access Plate, hand‑tighten the four 8 mm bolts, then **torque to the OEM spec** (refer to the Lincoln Electric manual; typically finger‑tight plus a quarter turn).  
14. Close and lock the Safety Interlock Door.  
15. Remove all LOTO devices, restore power to the robot and controller, and turn the main breaker back ON.  
16. Perform a **jog test**: move each axis (X, Y, Z, wrist roll, pitch, yaw) slowly using the teach pendant; verify smooth, noise‑free motion and no binding.  
17. Record the grease type, date, and any observations in the equipment maintenance log.  

## Completion Checklist
- [ ] Power and LOTO devices secured before work began.  
- [ ] Mechanical Unit Access Plate removed and all grease fittings identified.  
- [ ] Each fitting cleaned, greased, and excess removed.  
- [ ] All joints cycled to distribute grease.  
- [ ] Access plate reinstalled and bolts torqued per OEM guidance.  
- [ ] Safety interlock door closed and locked.  
- [ ] Power restored, LOTO devices removed.  
- [ ] Jog test completed with smooth motion on all axes.  
- [ ] Maintenance log updated with grease type, date, and technician initials.
