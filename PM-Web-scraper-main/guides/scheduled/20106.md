# Scheduled Maintenance Procedure: Holzma Saw (EQ ID 1361)

## Overview
This monthly preventive maintenance task for the Holzma Saw (EQ ID 1361) involves lubricating the scoring blade and side aligner, inspecting and tightening critical bolts on the Micro feeder/Counter and the roller table, and clearing any debris that may be blocking the duct path from the top hose. Completing these steps ensures proper function and integrity of key machine components.

## Tools & Parts Needed
- Grease gun (located inside the cabinet on the blade change side)
- Standard hand tools (for tightening bolts)
- No special parts.

## Safety
- Exercise caution when working underneath the roller table.
- Be mindful when climbing on top of the machine's table top.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1.  **Locate the grease gun:** Find the grease gun inside the cabinet on the blade change side of the machine.
2.  **Grease scoring blade and side aligner:** Using the grease gun, lubricate the Holzma scoring blade and the side aligner.
3.  **Check and tighten Micro feeder/Counter bolts:** Inspect all bolts on the Micro feeder/Counter. Tighten any loose bolts found.
4.  **Inspect and tighten roller table bolts (underneath):**
    a. Remove the side door to access the area underneath the roller table.
    b. Crawl underneath the roller table.
    c. Locate the bolts for the roller table.
    d. Verify that the bolts are positioned in-between the lines marked for their locations.
    e. Check and tighten all roller table bolts.
5.  **Inspect and clear top duct path for debris:**
    a. Climb on top of the machine's table top.
    b. Remove the clamp for the top hose.
    c. Look down inside the duct path to check for any debris blocking it.
    d. If debris is found, remove it.

## Completion Checklist
- Holzma scoring blade and side aligner greased.
- Bolts on Micro feeder/Counter checked and tightened.
- Bolts for roller table checked and tightened from underneath, ensuring they are between the marked lines.
- Top duct path checked for debris; debris removed if present.
