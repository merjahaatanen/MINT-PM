# Scheduled Maintenance Procedure: Fire extinguisher, Emergency light, Exit signs, Emergency radio (EQ ID 5373)

## Overview
The fire extinguisher, emergency lighting, exit signs, and emergency radio are life‑safety devices that must operate instantly during a power loss, fire, or other emergency. Regular verification ensures that extinguishing agents are pressurized, visual indicators are visible, batteries will sustain illumination for the required time, and the radio can broadcast alarms. This prevents delayed response, hidden damage, or complete loss of egress guidance that could endanger occupants and increase liability.

## Tools & Parts Needed
- Safety glasses and protective gloves (per PPE policy)  
- Disposable inspection tag or checklist pad  
- Small flat‑head screwdriver (for tamper seal inspection)  
- Battery tester or multimeter capable of measuring voltage and load for 12 V/24 V batteries  
- Clean, lint‑free cloth  
- Replacement tamper seal (if seal is broken)  
- New fire extinguisher pressure gauge (only if gauge is damaged)  
- Standard hand tools; no special parts unless damage is found  

## Safety
- Verify that the area is free of active fire hazards before beginning.  
- Do **not** discharge the fire extinguisher unless a controlled test is authorized.  
- Ensure the emergency lighting and exit sign circuits are isolated (use the building’s emergency power isolation switch) to avoid false alarms during functional tests.  
- Wear safety glasses and gloves when handling the extinguisher and battery terminals.  
- Follow lockout/tagout (LOTO) procedures for the emergency power supply if the building’s emergency system is powered down for testing.  

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. **Locate the fire extinguisher** mounted at the designated exit; confirm it is not obstructed by equipment, debris, or signage.  
2. **Visually inspect the extinguisher** for corrosion, dents, or rust on the cylinder body; note any damage.  
3. **Check the pressure gauge** – the needle must be in the green zone, between the “Low” and “Full” markings. If the needle is in the red or missing, tag the extinguisher for service.  
4. **Confirm the safety pin** is fully inserted and the tamper seal is intact; the seal should be unbroken, showing a continuous loop around the pin. Replace the seal if broken.  
5. **Inspect the nozzle and hose** for kinks, cracks, or blockage; ensure the hose is fully seated on the nozzle and the nozzle tip is free of debris.  
6. **Activate the 30‑second functional test on each exit sign**: press the test button on the sign (or via the building’s central test panel) and confirm the illuminated letters glow continuously for at least 30 seconds. Observe for flickering or dimness.  
7. **Examine exit signs for physical damage** – cracked housing, broken mounting brackets, or water intrusion. Replace any compromised signs.  
8. **Perform the 90‑minute battery test on the exit sign** (annual): power the sign from battery only by disconnecting the line voltage (use the isolation switch). Verify the sign remains fully illuminated for the full 90 minutes; record the start and end times. If illumination fades before 90 minutes, replace the battery pack.  
9. **Locate each emergency light unit** (typically ceiling‑mounted). Ensure the fixture is clean and unobstructed.  
10. **Conduct the 30‑second functional test on the emergency light**: activate the test button; confirm the lamp(s) come on bright and stay on for the full 30 seconds without flicker.  
11. **Visually inspect the emergency light housing** for cracks, corrosion, or missing screws; check the reflector and lamp for discoloration or soot.  
12. **Run the 90‑minute battery duration test** on the emergency light: isolate the unit from mains power, start the test, and confirm continuous illumination for at least 90 minutes. Note any dimming or early shutdown. Replace the battery if it does not meet the duration.  
13. **Check the emergency radio** mounted near the exit assembly: verify the antenna is intact and not corroded.  
14. **Perform a functional test of the emergency radio**: press the test button (or use the building’s emergency system test mode) and listen for a clear, audible alarm tone. Ensure volume is at the prescribed level per local code.  
15. **Inspect the radio housing** for water ingress, cracks, or loose wiring. Tighten any loose connection with a screwdriver.  
16. **Document all findings** on the inspection tag or checklist, noting any deficiencies and corrective actions taken.  
17. **Restore power** to the emergency lighting and exit sign circuits, re‑engage any LOTO devices, and verify that normal operation resumes (lights should return to standby mode).  

## Completion Checklist
- [ ] Fire extinguisher is visible, unobstructed, and free of physical damage.  
- [ ] Pressure gauge is in the green zone; no leaks observed.  
- [ ] Pin and tamper seal are present and intact; seal replaced if needed.  
- [ ] No damage or obstruction on extinguisher nozzle and hose.  
- [ ] All exit signs passed the 30‑second illumination test.  
- [ ] Exit signs show no physical damage; mounting secure.  
- [ ] 90‑minute battery test completed on each exit sign with full illumination for the entire period.  
- [ ] Emergency lights passed the 30‑second functional test.  
- [ ] No cracks, corrosion, or lamp degradation on emergency lights.  
- [ ] 90‑minute battery test completed on each emergency light with continuous illumination.  
- [ ] Emergency radio antenna intact; audible alarm tone clear during test.  
- [ ] Radio housing free of water damage or loose wiring.  
- [ ] All isolation switches returned to normal operation; LOTO removed.  
- [ ] Inspection tag/PM form signed and dated.
