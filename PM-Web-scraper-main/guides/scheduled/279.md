# Scheduled Maintenance Procedure: Punch Press - 120 Ton (EQ ID 473)

## Overview
Changing the clutch oil on the Aida PC‑5 120‑ton punch press removes contaminants that build up in the hydraulic clutch system, restores proper lubrication, and prevents premature wear of clutch plates and seals. Doing this annually (2024) ensures smooth ram engagement, maintains repeatable punch force, and reduces the risk of clutch slip or failure that could cause production downtime or damage to the press.

## Tools & Parts Needed
- PPE: safety glasses, hearing protection, steel‑toe shoes, gloves  
- Standard hand tools: combination wrenches (13 mm & 15 mm), ratchet‑socket set, screwdriver set  
- Torque wrench (for plug torquing as per OEM manual)  
- Oil catch pan (≥ 5 L) and funnel  
- Clean lint‑free rags  
- Aida‑approved clutch oil (as specified in the 2024 service bulletin)  
- New O‑ring for the drain plug (if existing O‑ring shows wear)  

## Safety
- **Lockout/Tagout:** De‑energize the press, place a lock on the main power switch, and tag the circuit breaker.  
- **Pressure Release:** Before opening any hydraulic component, engage the ram‐release lever and ensure the ram is fully withdrawn to the bottom dead center. Verify that hydraulic pressure is relieved by checking the pressure gauge reads zero.  
- **Isolation:** Close the main water/air supply to the press if they are routed through the clutch housing.  
- **Personal Protection:** Wear safety glasses, gloves, and steel‑toe shoes. Keep a clean workspace free of tripping hazards.  

## Step‑By‑Step Procedure
1. Verify lockout/tagout is applied and the ram is fully lowered; confirm no hydraulic pressure on the system.  
2. Remove the protective side guard and any chain‑safety covers that obscure the clutch housing.  
3. Locate the clutch oil reservoir on the left side of the drive unit; it is identified by a rectangular metal cover with the “Clutch Oil” label.  
4. Place the oil catch pan beneath the reservoir drain plug (12 mm hex).  
5. Loosen and remove the drain plug using a 13 mm wrench; allow all oil to drain completely (≈ 3–5 min).  
6. Inspect the drain plug O‑ring for cracks, flattening, or wear; replace with a new O‑ring if any defect is seen.  
7. Clean the drain plug threads and the plug seating surface with a lint‑free rag.  
8. Re‑install the drain plug and torque to the value specified in the Aida PC‑5 service manual (typically “hand‑tight + ¼ turn”).  
9. Remove the fill plug (15 mm) on the opposite side of the reservoir; position the funnel in the opening.  
10. Using a clean pump or gravity feed, add fresh clutch oil until the oil level reaches the “Full” mark on the sight glass (or the top of the fill port).  
11. Replace the fill plug and torque to the manual‑specified value.  
12. Wipe any spilled oil from the reservoir, guard, and surrounding components.  
13. Re‑install the side guard and any removed safety covers.  
14. Remove lockout/tagout devices, restore power, and re‑pressurize the hydraulic system.  
15. Perform a low‑speed test cycle: start the press, run the ram at 10 % of full speed for three strokes, and listen for abnormal noises or clutch slip.  
16. Observe the clutch engagement point; ensure the ram stops precisely at the bottom dead center without hunting.  

## Completion Checklist
- [ ] Machine is locked out and pressure fully released before work begins.  
- [ ] All old clutch oil removed and reservoir drained completely.  
- [ ] Drain plug O‑ring inspected and replaced if necessary.  
- [ ] New clutch oil added to the correct level; sight glass shows “Full.”  
- [ ] Drain and fill plugs torqued per OEM manual.  
- [ ] Guard and safety covers re‑installed.  
- [ ] Lockout/tagout removed, power restored, and system re‑pressurized.  
- [ ] Low‑speed test completed with smooth clutch engagement and no leaks.  
- [ ] Work area cleaned and oil spill free.  

*Mark the work order complete once all items above are verified.*
