# Scheduled Maintenance Procedure: Cabinet doors all of BLA (EQ ID 1881)

## Overview
This procedure ensures that all cabinet doors in the BLA facility, specifically in first and second floor sink and lunchroom areas, are properly aligned and spaced. The goal is to prevent doors from being slanted or having large gaps in the middle, ensuring they are equally spaced out. Adjustments are to be made if necessary to maintain proper alignment.

## Tools & Parts Needed
- Standard hand tools; no special parts.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1.  Go to the first floor and second floor of the BLA facility.
2.  Locate all cabinet doors in the areas by sinks and in lunchrooms on both floors.
3.  For each cabinet door identified, perform a visual inspection to check the following conditions:
    *   The door is not slanted.
    *   There is no big gap in the middle of the door or between the door and the cabinet frame.
    *   The door is equally spaced out from adjacent doors or the cabinet frame.
4.  If any cabinet door is found to be slanted, has a big gap in the middle, or is not equally spaced, adjust it as needed to correct the condition and ensure proper alignment and spacing.

## Completion Checklist
*   All cabinet doors on the first floor and second floor by sinks and lunchroom areas have been inspected.
*   All inspected cabinet doors are not slanted.
*   All inspected cabinet doors do not have a big gap in the middle.
*   All inspected cabinet doors are equally spaced out.
*   Any necessary adjustments have been made.
