# Scheduled Maintenance Procedure: Vertical Panel Saw (EQ ID 327)

## Overview  
The vertical panel saw uses a hydraulic system to drive the cutting carriage and to position the saw blade. Over time hydraulic oil degrades and contaminants collect in the filter, which can cause reduced cutting precision, increased wear on the pump and seals, and ultimately unplanned downtime. Changing the oil and filter on schedule keeps the hydraulic circuit clean, maintains proper pump pressure, and protects the drive components from premature failure.

## Tools & Parts Needed
- PPE: safety glasses, steel‑toe shoes, cut‑resistant gloves, hearing protection  
- Standard hand tools: ratchet & socket set (incl. 10 mm & 13 mm), open‑end wrenches, screwdrivers  
- Oil drain pan (minimum 10 L capacity)  
- Funnel with hose (for clean fill)  
- New hydraulic oil (A&S recommended grade, quantity per manual)  
- New hydraulic filter cartridge (model per OEM service bulletin)  
- Filter wrench or strap wrench (if required for the filter housing)  

## Safety
- **Lockout/Tagout:** Disconnect main power, lock the electrical disconnect, and place a tag indicating maintenance in progress.  
- **Relieve pressure:** Before opening any hydraulic fittings, open the system bleed valve to release stored pressure.  
- **Containment:** Position the drain pan beneath the reservoir and use absorbent pads to catch any spills.  
- **PPE:** Wear eye protection and gloves at all times; use proper lifting techniques when moving the oil container.  

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Verify the machine is fully powered down and the LOTO devices are in place.  
2. Locate the hydraulic reservoir on the rear‑right side of the saw; note the sight‑glass level indicator.  
3. Open the hydraulic bleed valve (near the pump) using a 10 mm wrench to relieve system pressure; close it immediately after oil stops flowing.  
4. Position the oil drain pan beneath the reservoir drain plug (bottom of the tank).  
5. Remove the 13 mm drain plug with a socket wrench; allow all oil to completely drain into the pan (≈ 6‑8 L).  
6. Once drainage stops, reinstall the drain plug and snug it finger‑tight (final torque per OEM manual).  
7. Locate the filter housing on the side of the reservoir; remove the retaining bolt (usually 10 mm) holding the old cartridge.  
8. Pull the used filter straight out, discard it, and inspect the housing for dirt or metal particles.  
9. Install the new filter cartridge, ensuring the flow arrow points toward the pump, and secure the housing bolt to the specified torque.  
10. Using a clean funnel, pour the recommended hydraulic oil into the fill port until the sight‑glass reads the “Full” line.  
11. Replace the fill cap and tighten securely.  
12. Open the bleed valve again; start the hydraulic pump by briefly activating the saw’s power‑on switch (no blade engagement).  
13. Run the pump until a steady stream of clean oil exits the bleed valve, indicating all air has been purged.  
14. Close the bleed valve firmly with a 10 mm wrench.  
15. Inspect the oil level again; add oil if the sight‑glass has dropped below the “Full” line.  
16. Remove the LOTO devices, restore power, and perform a short test cycle (open/close the blade carriage) to confirm normal operation and absence of leaks.  

## Completion Checklist
- ☐ Machine powered down, LOTO applied, and pressure relieved.  
- ☐ All old hydraulic oil drained and properly disposed of.  
- ☐ Drain plug reinstalled and torqued per OEM guidance.  
- ☐ Old filter removed; housing cleaned and new filter installed with correct orientation.  
- ☐ Reservoir filled to the “Full” sight‑glass line with correct oil type.  
- ☐ Air fully bled from the system; bleed valve closed securely.  
- ☐ No oil leaks observed at drain plug, filter housing, or bleed valve after test run.  
- ☐ Test cycle completed successfully; hydraulic functions normal.  
- ☐ Work area cleaned and LOTO devices removed.  

*Mark work order complete after all checklist items are verified.*
