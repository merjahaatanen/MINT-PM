# Operator Troubleshooting Checklist: Saw, Horizontal, Holzma (EQ ID 1361)

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Symptoms | Troubleshooting Steps | Avg. Fix Time | When to Call Maintenance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sheet Feeding, Gripper & Clamping Issues | - Material not going in or hitting something<br>- Gap closer sticking up or bent<br>- Pusher arm leaning, bent, stuck, or not retracting<br>- Sheets going in crooked<br>- Machine crashing into sheets<br>- Micro infeed not level<br>- Micro infeed turning hard<br>- "Program fence moves not out of area of pressure beam" error<br>- Saw stops midway or past cut line<br>- Finger pusher pushing too many sheets or getting stuck<br>- Gripper arm appears damaged or loose<br>- Sheets are chipping or scratched by grippers/arms<br>- Air leak sound from grippers/hoses<br>- Cardboard stuck on clamps<br>- Saw guide not squared, causing non‑squared cuts<br>- Saw taking 2 sheets at a time when set for 1<br>- Material vibrating during cut, causing chipping<br>- Material slipping from grippers causing undersized pieces<br>- Machine will not infeed sheet (potential magnetic strip failure)<br>- Guide moved causing cuts up to 1/16 undersize (WO 19912)<br>- Saw guide not properly squared, resulting in non‑squared cuts (WO 18505)<br>- Clamp pressure too high, damaging material (WO 17612) | - Inspect Gap Closers: Look for bent or lifted gap closers. Carefully try to push them back down if slightly bent.<br>- Check for Obstructions: Look for foreign material (plastic film, fall‑off, cardboard) in the path of the sheets, micro infeed, or clamps. Remove carefully.<br>- Examine Pusher/Gripper Arms: Visually check if pusher arms or grippers are leaning, bent, or out of alignment. If slightly out, gently try to straighten them if safe to do so.<br>- Check Micro Infeed Sensors: Ensure sensors (especially the blue ones) are clean and free of dust or particles. Use an air nozzle to clean them.<br>- Perform Air System Bleed: If the machine has an “air” system, turn off the main air supply, bleed the system, then turn air back on.<br>- Manually Move Micro Arm: If safe, gently try to move the micro arm back and forth and clean its track.<br>- Adjust Hold Down Pressure: If chipping/vibration occurs, try increasing the hold down pressure regulator slightly (e.g., from 5.5 bar to 6 bar) after consulting lead.<br>- Power Cycle: If an error related to infeed/retraction persists, try a full machine shutdown and restart.<br>- Clean Grippers: Use a scraper and lacquer thinner to remove plastic or wax buildup from under the grippers.<br>- Inspect guide alignment; adjust and realign guide as needed (WO 19912)<br>- Verify and adjust clamp pressure regulator if material damage observed (WO 17612) | ~1.2 hrs (31 work orders) | - Gap closer cannot be pushed down or is broken<br>- Pusher or arm is bent, twisted, or broken and cannot be straightened by hand<br>- Micro‑infeed cylinder is stuck, leaking, or damaged<br>- Stripped bolts or broken hardware on grippers/pushers remain after tightening attempts<br>- Persistent air leak from grippers or hoses after checking connections<br>- Saw guide remains out of square after basic checks<br>- Errors persist after cleaning sensors and power cycling<br>- Magnetic strip or cover is broken or detached |
| Scoring Blade / Belt Issues | - Scoring blade sounds bogged down or makes grinding noise<br>- Scoring belt teeth missing or belt ripped<br>- Scoring blade does not move up/down<br>- Error messages related to scoring blade (e.g., “Scoring blade malfunction”, “Scoring blade drive not functioning”)<br>- Scoring belt broken or missing<br>- Material wedged behind scoring blade cover<br>- Scoring blade lock hot, burning smell (WO 2364) | - Inspect scoring belt for missing teeth or damage; replace if torn or broken.<br>- Clean area around scoring belt and surrounding components.<br>- Remove any foreign material (plastic, phenolic strips) that may be wedged behind the scoring blade cover.<br>- Verify scoring blade is free to move; manually raise/lower if necessary.<br>- Check scoring motor and associated overload circuit; reset if tripped.<br>- After replacement, run a test cut to confirm smooth operation.<br>- Check scoring blade lock temperature; replace if overheating (WO 2364) | ~1.0 hrs (7 work orders) | - Scoring belt requires replacement<br>- Scoring blade still does not move after cleaning<br>- Scoring motor overload trips repeatedly<br>- Repeated scoring errors after basic fixes |
| Dust Collection / Vacuum Issues | - Vacuum suction weak or not working<br>- Excessive dust on floor or machine surface<br>- Dust collector clogged with plastic film or debris<br>- Bottom suction weak<br>- Dust accumulation in blade compartment<br>- Top suction not strong | - Inspect dust collection ducts and 8″ ducting for plastic film or fall‑off; remove any blockage.<br>- Clean the top section of the dust collection system; clear debris from blade compartment.<br>- Replace or clean filters if dirty.<br>- Verify suction hose connections are secure and not leaking.<br>- Run the machine briefly to confirm suction restored. | ~1.4 hrs (7 work orders) | - Blockage cannot be cleared with manual cleaning<br>- Air hose or duct is damaged and needs replacement<br>- Filters require replacement |
| Main Blade / Motor / Belt Issues | - Main blade does not cut or cuts very short<br>- Main belt missing, ripped, or broken<br>- Main motor failure message<br>- Blade holder/lock extremely hot<br>- Blade does not return home after cut<br>- Main blade belt broken; blade stays up (WO 14285)<br>- Small belt snapped (WO 16351)<br>- RPM belt missing teeth or ripped (WO 17193)<br>- Clicking noise possibly belt issue (WO 18825) | - Turn off power and verify main blade belt is intact; replace belt if ripped (part S8M‑1256).<br>- Check blade mounting flanges and set screws; tighten or replace if loose.<br>- Reset overload circuit in electrical panel; verify motor power.<br>- If blade holder is hot, allow to cool, then check for material wedged behind cover and remove.<br>- Replace broken main blade belt (WO 14285).<br>- Replace snapped small belt (WO 16351).<br>- Replace RPM belt with correct teeth (WO 17193).<br>- Inspect belt for wear; replace if damaged (WO 18825).<br>- After repairs, perform a test cut and ensure blade returns to home position. | ~1.5 hrs (7 work orders) | - Main belt or motor needs replacement<br>- Blade flange or set screw broken<br>- Motor overload persists after reset |
| Electrical / Computer Issues | - Machine has no power<br>- BIOS update required<br>- Computer shows “ERROR COMPACTING DATABASE – NOT ENOUGH SPACE ON DISK”<br>- Disk space errors stop cutting<br>- Machine does not start after reset<br>- On/off switch loose and cannot turn machine on/off (WO 18486)<br>- Main power switch fails to turn off machine; operators using breaker (WO 18617) | - Verify power switch connections at rear and re‑connect if loose.<br>- Perform a BIOS update as instructed by Stiles technical support.<br>- Replace CMOS battery (CR2032) if the computer clock is incorrect.<br>- Free disk space on the D: drive by deleting old job files or archives.<br>- Power‑cycle the machine (shut down, wait 1 min, restart).<br>- Verify power switch operation; replace if defective (WO 18486, WO 18617). | ~1.1 hrs (7 work orders) | - Power switch or wiring fault persists<br>- CMOS battery replacement required<br>- Disk‑space issue cannot be resolved by user deletion |
| Table / Support Structural Issues | - Supporting leg shifting or moving left<br>- Plate support broken<br>- Table top plates broken or cracked<br>- Lift table bracket bent or missing<br>- Table top missing slide bearings<br>- Lift table extension repaired previously<br>- Table top corner chip repaired with filler (WO 12162)<br>- Missing slide bearings replaced (WO 15694)<br>- Loose screw on plate causing scratches (WO 19174) | - Tighten or adjust supporting legs as needed.<br>- Replace broken plate parts (e.g., part 34‑398‑24472).<br>- Replace damaged table top plates and reinstall rollers.<br>- Replace bent lift table brackets (e.g., part 34‑412‑87400) and re‑install cam/PRS components.<br>- Install missing roller bearings on table top.<br>- Repair table top chip with filler, sand smooth (WO 12162).<br>- Replace missing slide bearings (WO 15694).<br>- Tighten or replace stripped screw causing scratches (WO 19174). | ~1.2 hrs (7 work orders) | - Structural component is cracked or broken and requires part replacement |
| Sensor / Photo‑Eye / Calibration Issues | - Photo eye covered with plastic<br>- Program fence does not move or fails to calibrate<br>- Errors like “Program fence moves not out of area of pressure beam”<br>- Sensor stuck or dirty<br>- Calibration fails repeatedly<br>- Plastic covering left side sensor (WO 19131) | - Clean photo eye and surrounding area with compressed air.<br>- Remove dust from program fence and surrounding components.<br>- Verify sensor wiring; replace sensor if damaged (e.g., part 4‑008‑61‑011).<br>- Perform a full power cycle and re‑run machine calibration.<br>- Remove obstruction and clean sensor (WO 19131).<br>- If error persists, schedule a Stiles tech visit for sensor replacement. | ~1.6 hrs (5 work orders) | - Sensor or photo‑eye needs replacement<br>- Calibration still fails after cleaning and power cycle |
| Gripper Arm Failures | - Gripper arm crashed or fell to floor<br>- Gripper arm chipping material<br>- Air hose on gripper leaking or broken<br>- Magnetic strip or limit switch damaged<br>- Finger pusher pushing multiple sheets and getting stuck (WO 11308)<br>- Twisted rods on micro pusher; cylinder replaced (WO 11354) | - Replace the broken gripper arm assembly.<br>- Replace damaged air hose (use part 34‑412‑87400 or similar).<br>- Install new magnetic strip and cover (part 34‑486‑02056).<br>- Replace limit switch if broken.<br>- Replace worn cylinder rubber attachment (WO 11308).<br>- Replace twisted micro pusher cylinder (WO 11354).<br>- Re‑assemble and test gripper operation. | ~1.5 hrs (6 work orders) | - Gripper arm is broken beyond simple realignment<br>- Multiple components (air hose, magnetic strip, limit switch) need replacement |
